Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Simplecell Analytics is a private tool used only by its owner. With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own YouTube channel analytics (read-only).

  • It does not collect, sell, or share personal information about any other person.
  • The analytics it reads are stored only on the owner's own computer and are not shared with third parties.
  • It cannot change, upload, or delete anything on the channel.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Simplecell Analytics's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
